# AI provider comparison

Generated by `node apps/web/scripts/compare-providers.mjs` on 2026-10-11, against the real routes on a local dev server (port 3051, dev auth, AI_MOCK unset). Each config ran 16 role-play turns (PM-017 and CL-102, 4 reflective and 4 lecturing scripted learner turns each), 4 debriefs, and 12 drill answers (6 drills, one good and one weak answer each). Single run per config, so treat latency as indicative.

## Status and caveats

- **The OpenAI defaults are not measured yet.** `AI_PROVIDER=openai` puts the observer and coach on `gpt-6.1-sol`, but the OpenAI account has no paid credits. Every Sol and Astra call returned `429 credit_balance_exhausted`. I rechecked over 20 minutes and nothing changed.
- **Luna stood in for every role.** The free tier lets the account call `gpt-6-luna` only, so the OpenAI config here runs Luna everywhere.
- **The free tier throttled the Luna run.** Its limits are 10 requests per minute and 50 per day. Two things went wrong because of that:
  - CL-102 lecturing turn 4 got a mid-stream rate-limit error, so that transcript's debrief ran on a "(no reply)" turn.
  - The last drill answer was never sent.
- **Luna's latencies are inflated.** The SDK retries 429s once, which inflates Luna's observer and coach timings. Don't read them as Luna's real speed.
- **To finish the OpenAI side:**
  1. Add credits at platform.openai.com. This also lifts the free-tier rate limits.
  2. Run `node apps/web/scripts/compare-providers.mjs --configs openai,openai-luna --from-json <previous.json> --json <out.json>`.
  3. That run adds the Sol rows and refreshes this file. The hand-written block is kept.
- **Estimate in the meantime.** Pricing Luna's observer and coach token counts at 6.1 Sol rates gives about $0.011 (observer) and $0.012 (coach) per debrief. The default OpenAI session would then be about $0.024. That's an estimate, not a measurement, and medium effort on Sol will likely use more reasoning tokens than Luna did.
- **These checks only catch hard failures.** All three configs passed nearly all of them. The checks don't separate "realistic" from "fine", so read the quotes below.
- **Two shared misses.** PM-017's reflective script ends on "What would make month-end feel workable again?" Sonnet and Luna both answered it by raising the broken template, which the observer codes as sustain (-1). For Sonnet, that tied the lecturing run, so that check failed. Sonnet broke the four-sentence rule five times.

## Recommendation

- **OpenAI's saving comes only from Luna, and Anthropic has a model at the same price.**
  - Luna costs $0.10 / $0.50 per 1M tokens, the same as `claude-haiku-5-5`.
  - 6.1 Sol costs the same as Sonnet 5.5.
  - Astra costs more than Opus.
  - So most of the saving comes from taking Opus and Sonnet off roles that don't need them. Switching vendor adds little on top.
- **Do now, on Anthropic, with no new vendor: session cost drops from $0.056 to $0.022 (-61%).**
  - Role-player and drill coach move to Haiku 5.5. Haiku passed the same checks, with the same or lower latency.
  - Coach moves from Opus to Sonnet 5.5. It passed 12/12 and debriefs got about 1.6s faster.
  - Haiku's role-play is a little plainer than Sonnet's but stays in voice (see the quotes).
- **Then try the observer on Luna, after adding OpenAI credits.**
  - The observer becomes the largest line item, at about $0.009 per session.
  - Luna coded all six observer checks right, for about $0.0006.
  - `AI_PROVIDER_OBSERVER=openai` with `AI_OPENAI_MODEL_OBSERVER=gpt-6-luna` would bring a session to about $0.012.
- **Keep the coach on Anthropic for now.**
  - Luna's debriefs are usable but flatter. They wrap suggested wording in quotation marks and are less specific about why a moment mattered.
  - Rerun the comparison with Sol before moving the coach to OpenAI.
- **Use Luna everywhere ($0.002 a session) only if cost matters more than polish.**
  - It passed the checks.
  - Its role-play is good but more articulate and less "Darnell" than Sonnet's.

## What the checks are

- Role-player, per reply: no meta talk (AI, simulation, scenario, MI terms), at most 4 sentences, no asterisks, one paragraph, not empty. 16 replies x 5 checks.
- Observer: the scripted complex reflection (learner turn 2 of each reflective script) is coded complex-reflection; the scripted advice (learner turn 2 of each lecturing script) is coded advice-without-permission or confront; and for each scenario the reflective transcript ends at a higher counterpart intensity than the lecturing one. 6 checks.
- Coach, per debrief: the JSON parses with all fields; no "correct"/"incorrect" outside the suggested learner wording; no wrong-setting nouns (clinical never says employee, leader, manager or direct report; workplace never says clinician or "your patient"). 4 debriefs x 3 checks.
- Drill coach: `move` matches the expected code for each answer (12). Persuading answers accept advice-without-permission or confront, since a correction is a confront under the observer's rules. Not counted in the table but recorded: hitTarget is true for good answers and false for weak ones, plus the same parse, wording and correct/incorrect checks as the coach.
